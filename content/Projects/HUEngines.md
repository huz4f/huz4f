### The Engineering Methodology

A structured, 5-stage engineering lifecycle:

1. **Human Intent** — Understanding the business objective, economic leverage, and operational friction before writing a single line of code.
2. **Intelligence** — Data analysis, workflow topology mapping, and system architecture design.
3. **Systems** — Bespoke full-stack engineering across high-throughput web/mobile platforms, deterministic backend engines, and secure data pipelines.
4. **Execution** — Zero-downtime deployment, security hardening, automated failover, and autonomous operation.
5. **Business Outcome** — Permanent intellectual property ownership and compounding enterprise valuation.
