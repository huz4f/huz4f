# 📷 Photography Upload Directory

Welcome to your personal photography directory!

## How to add your own photos:
1. Simply copy/drop your `.jpg`, `.png`, `.webp`, or `.avif` photo files into this folder (`static/photos/`).
2. Update the photo list in `content/pages/about.md` (or add new items to the `PHOTOS_DATA` array).
3. Your photos will automatically render in the stunning Pinterest / Instagram gallery on your `/about/` page!

## Supported Formats:
- JPEG (`.jpg`, `.jpeg`)
- PNG (`.png`)
- WebP (`.webp`)
- AVIF (`.avif`)
